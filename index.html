About

Chhath Puja is a four-day festival dedicated to Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya, observed with fasting, purity and gratitude. Every year our society comes together to celebrate it as one family. Everyone is welcome to join, help and receive prasad.

Four-Day Schedule

Day 1 · Nahay Khay[Date]

Holy bath and a simple sattvik meal of lauki-bhaat and chana dal.

Day 2 · Kharna[Date]

Day-long fast, broken after sunset with kheer-roti prasad. Nirjala fast begins.

Day 3 · Sandhya Arghya[Date] · Sunset about [Time]

Evening offering to the setting sun at the ghat with the whole society.

Day 4 · Usha Arghya[Date] · Sunrise about [Time]

Morning offering to the rising sun, followed by parana and prasad distribution.

Guidelines

  • Please keep the ghat and surroundings clean and plastic-free.
  • Vrati families are requested to arrive 30 minutes before arghya time.
  • Prasad is strictly sattvik: no onion, garlic or non-veg anywhere near the puja area.
  • Please keep footwear outside the puja area.
